### Founder requirement for the opening (also in `docs/DEVELOPMENT_ROADMAP.md` section 2a)

**Founder requirement (stated 2026-10-26; implemented in code and tests the same day; NOT live).** Every call opens by (1) introducing the **configured business** and the **agent** by name, (2) giving the **disclosure** (that the caller is speaking with an AI assistant, and the recording notice the business needs), and (3) mentioning a **special or promotion only if that business has approved one**, as exact approved text. The business name, agent name, disclosure wording and any special are per-business configuration, never invented or reworded by the model, and a special never carries a price or a commitment. In code all three are now configurable through an optional `opening` block; the live config has none, so live calls still use the original notice (`docs/PRODUCT_CONTINUITY.md` section 4, stage 2). None of the sources below states a disclosure; one asks the question and does not answer it, so the wording is the business's and a legal check's to settle.

## 3. A proposed question order (for discussion; not approved)

1. Greeting, AI and recording disclosure, and the consent question (today: the consent step exists; the disclosure wording is the business's to approve).
2. Service type (standard, deep, move-out, or "just a quote").
3. Property details (bedrooms, bathrooms or rooms, size if known, pets).
4. Frequency (one-time or recurring).
5. Preferred date and time window.
6. Contact: name, callback number (read back), service address.
7. Access method (no codes).
8. Read-back of the job and the number, then the caller's yes or no.
9. Later, in simulation first: a quote or estimate under the business's policy, the customer's explicit yes, a slot, a verified booking.
10. At any point: the fixed human-fallback triggers.

The assistant keeps asking **one named field at a time, chosen by code** (the existing design), so each addition is a configuration change to the skill plus tests, not new conversation logic.
